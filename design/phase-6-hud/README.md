# Phase 6 — Game HUD (action history, players, actions, deed card) — variants F1–F3

Status: **PICKED: F3** — built 2026-09-27 (`components/hud/*`, `app/room/[code]/GameView.tsx`).

The background is a real capture of the live board (`board-bg.png`, from demo room `DEMO42`
seeded in the dev database). HUD panels are HTML overlays generated by `build-variants.cjs`
and rendered at 1600×1000 with headless Edge.

## Shared content (every variant)
- **Players:** pawn color, name, "(you)", BOT and IN JAIL tags, cash, a short holdings line
  ("4 cities · 1 hotel"), and a mango highlight on whoever's turn it is.
- **History:** the engine log grouped by turn ("TURN 14 · MAREK"). Each event has an icon
  tile and an amount pill: mint for +, pink for −, lilac when it goes to the jackpot. The
  current decision is highlighted. Filters: All · Money · Moves.
- **Actions:** dice readout, contextual primary buttons (Buy / Decline / Roll / End turn),
  and secondary Build · Mortgage · Jail card (these open a picker on your cities).
- **Table info:** jackpot pot and room-rules chips. "⚙ Rules" opens the lobby rules sheet read-only.

## Variants
- **F1 — Side rails** (`variant-F1-hud.png`): players plus table info on the left, a tall
  History column on the right, and a floating action bar at the bottom center. The history
  is always visible and long. The board gets smaller.
- **F2 — Top bar + console** (`variant-F2-hud.png`): player cards across the top, and one
  bottom console with the turn and actions on the left and History on the right. The board
  stays big and centered, but only the last few events show without scrolling.
- **F3 — Deed card + tabs** (`variant-F3-hud.png`): player cards on top, and a tabbed right
  card (History · My cities · Rules). Buying happens on a property deed card (full rent
  ladder, set owners, cash after buying), shown in the middle of the board. A compact turn
  pill sits bottom-left. It answers "should I buy?" best, but it has the most parts.

Mix-and-match is possible, e.g. F1's rails with F3's deed card for buy decisions.
